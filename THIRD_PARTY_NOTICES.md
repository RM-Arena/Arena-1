Exact versions and transitive dependencies are recorded in `bun.lock`,
`uv.lock` and the Cargo lockfiles. Desktop bundles retain Electron's
`LICENSE`, `LICENSES.chromium.html` and the standalone Python runtime notices.
The entries above are an index; the licenses distributed by each component
remain authoritative.

Desktop packaging also copies available complete JavaScript and Rust dependency
license and notice files into `runtime/app/third_party_licenses/`, with a
name/version/license index. This collection includes build-time packages as
well as runtime packages. Python package notices remain with their installed
distribution metadata. These third-party terms are not replaced by Arena-1's
non-commercial learning restriction.

MP4 export runs the FFmpeg executable from the locked imageio-ffmpeg wheel as
a separate process. Its upstream binaries and build information are maintained
at https://github.com/imageio/imageio-binaries/tree/master/ffmpeg; FFmpeg source
releases are available at https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ and libx264 source at
https://code.videolan.org/videolan/x264. Complete GPL v2/v3 license texts are
retained in `docs/third_party/ffmpeg/`. Desktop preparation also records the
actual binary's `-L` and `-buildconf` output under
`runtime/app/third_party_licenses/ffmpeg/`. The FFmpeg binary and codecs retain
their own licenses, independently of Arena-1's original code and assets.

## Models, sound and rules

The original robot and field models are generated by the Blender programs in
`assets/blender/`. Synthetic broadcast tracks are generated by
`assets/audio/compose_broadcast.py`. Original project material is covered by
the project's non-commercial learning license.

The supplied recordings `home.mp3`, `intro-free.m4a`, `summoning-glory.m4a`
and `summoning-glory.mp3`, the cue themes `match-theme`, `pick-theme`,
`results-theme`, `mvp-theme` and `highlight-theme` (each shipped as `.m4a`
and `.mp3`), and announcer audio are external material, not relicensed as
original Arena-1 work. The maintainer has confirmed redistribution permission
for these supplied tracks. Any additional use of external recordings must
comply with the permission of their respective rights holders.

Per-machine overrides (`<cue>-custom.m4a` / `.mp3`) are ignored by git and are
not distributed with the project; whoever installs one is responsible for its
permissions.

The simulated rules and dimensions are documented in
`docs/RULES_IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT.md`; the original competition rules PDF
is not distributed. RoboMaster and related marks belong to their respective
owners. Arena-1 is an independent project and does not claim endorsement.
