- **Engineer (slot 2)**: cannot shoot; shuttles between your resource zone and assembly zone to assemble tech cores. Every success adds a coin payment to the team every 10 s; the first level-2 success raises the team level cap to 7, the first level-3 success raises it to 10 and gives 25% defense, and level 4 raises defense to 50% and restores 2000 base HP. Assembly progress is in `observation["assemblies"][your team]`. The engineer has 50% defense for the first 180 s and is invulnerable in the resource zone and in the assembly zone (the latter for at most 180 s in total).
